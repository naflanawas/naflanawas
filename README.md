## About Me

I'm Nafla, a Computer Science graduate from the University of Westminster, UK. My interests center on research and product development, with a particular focus on exploring emerging applications of artificial intelligence.

I have hands-on experience across backend and full-stack development, including Node.js, PHP, and the MERN stack, along with mobile and front-end development using Flutter and React. I enjoy translating research ideas into practical, well-engineered products and am currently expanding my work into AI-driven systems.
